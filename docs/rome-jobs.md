# Rome: every job in the city

A working list of the trades, offices and callings of the city of Rome from the late Republic to
the 6th century. It is drawn from the occupations named on Roman inscriptions (tombstones and
guild dedications collected in the *Corpus Inscriptionum Latinarum*), from the legal and literary
sources, and from the late-antique guild and church records.

- **Latin forms:** the usual ones. Where a trade had a guild (*collegium*), the guild is noted.
- **Ages:**
  - R: the Republic, about 50 BC.
  - E: the Empire, about AD 150.
  - L: the late Empire, about AD 330.
  - F: the emptied city of AD 552.
- **Game:** the jobs marked ● walk the city as citizens you can talk to (`content/rome/people.json`).
  The rest are here for later.
