# Sprint 1: Greece feels like home

**Goal:** the Shore and Porphyry's villa become places you want to linger in, and the groundwork for
every later world (music per zone, the fidelity ladder) is in place.

## Stories (GitHub milestone "Sprint 1: Greece feels like home")
1. Each zone has its own track (the Shore keeps its theme; Sicily gets a warmer lyre track; crossfade on travel).
2. Places sound alive (waves, wind, fire on the Shore; cicadas, waves, fountain in Sicily). Includes #11.
3. Fidelity rises world by world (a per-world render size and model detail; Greece stays 320x180).
4. Sit and watch the sunset (benches; F to sit, the view settles, the music swells).
5. Life in the places (swallows, a cat on the villa steps, moths at the lamps, swaying trees).
6. Keepsakes to find (three in the villa, each with a one-line story; listed on the tablet).
7. #39 Fine-tune from BB1's playtest notes.
8. #46 Retire the old 3D card-game code.

## Playtest checklist for BB1
- Does each place sound and feel different? Does the Sicily track fit?
- Did you sit on a bench? Did you want to stay a while?
- Did you find the keepsakes without being told where?
- Anything annoying, confusing or ugly?
