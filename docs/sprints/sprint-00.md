# Sprint 0: finding the game (2026-09-25)

## What shipped
- A world of places: the Shore (a Majula-like hub, 17 gateways in reading order) and Porphyry's
  villa at Lilybaeum, Sicily, 270 AD.
- Porphyry teaches: a seated figure who looks up at you, a six-point lesson, a check that fills your
  tablet only from your own words, memory between visits, mastery lighting the gateway and raising
  a column of the Shore's temple.
- The map of the world in 270 AD, busts of Aristotle and Plotinus, Porphyry's Tree, the real book.
- Controls: WASD, Shift sprint, Q/E turn, F use, mouse look from the start; 55 tests.

## Retro
- **Didn't work:** silent puzzles ("too esoteric"), then text-heavy guided stories ("useless"), then
  a stone corridor that didn't match the mood. Each was built in full before BB1 saw a slice.
- **Worked:** the retro look and the Majula-like music; the author answering from the real text;
  places that look and feel like ancient Greece; a teacher with a goal.
- **Change:** show a small slice before building a whole module; plan in sprints with a playtest gate;
  one place per teacher (Aristotle has three books); only launch visible test runs on request; a
  crosshair once ate all mouse movement, so there is now a test that nothing on screen catches the
  mouse at the centre.
