# Sprint 6: every Greek room open

**Goal:** the rest of the Greek reading list gets its places, built like sprints 4 and 5 (a teacher who
answers from a public-domain translation, a stone or a beat for every point, the place lost to the world
and mended piece by piece, its music changing with each piece), out of the set pieces made in rounds 1
and 2. No new models; a few new things held in the hand, two maps, and a few sound layers from existing
files. Asked by BB1 (2026-09-28): "just add content, we will fix all during a pass".

## Rooms
Each room has six points: three or four are stones with a scene round them, and two or three are
beats, played in the first person.

The four plays of the theatre (9-12) each include the theatre of Dionysus with their chorus in the
orchestra. New heroes for the beats: Agamemnon, Oedipus, Antigone, Pentheus, Croesus, a Spartan; new
things held: a staff, a handful of dust, a thyrsus. Sappho's poem about the loveliest thing on earth
(LP 16) was found after Wharton; its point is given in plain words, not quoted.

## Known rough edges (for the fix pass)
- Nothing in this sprint was screenshotted; placements are from the set pieces' coordinates only.
- Some corpora carry editor's notes (Sophocles' footnotes, a few of Burnet's) and Wharton's Sappho misses
  fragments whose literal prose he did not set in italics.
- Euripides' milk from the earth needs water, which is not one of that room's pieces.
- The olive rejection in the Academy grove was left undone at BB1's request.

## Playtest checklist for BB1
- The Shore: are all seventeen Greek gateways open?
- In each room: is every stone and beat easy to find? Does anything float, sink or block the way?
- Play every beat. Is the hero's view right? Is the pace of one line at a time right?
- Lost to whole: does each piece that comes back show and sound?
- Do the teachers answer well from their books?
